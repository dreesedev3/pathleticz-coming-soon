Get in
the game.

Every gift puts a student in the game: face to face with the professionals who run the sports industry, building the skills and portfolio to compete for a place in it.

When you donate to Pathleticz, you support the materials, prizes, food, and transportation that keep programs accessible for students and families.

Pathleticz is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 42-2273307). Gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Questions about giving? Reach us at give@pathleticz.com.

Give through your fund

If you hold a donor-advised fund (DAF), you can recommend a grant to Pathleticz the same way you would for any other charity:

  1. Sign in to your DAF account and start a new grant recommendation.
  2. Search for Pathleticz, or enter our EIN: 42-2273307.
  3. Choose your amount and submit. Your fund sends the grant once it's approved.

Need a hand with the process? Email give@pathleticz.com.

Double your assist

Many employers match the charitable gifts their employees make, so one donation can count twice.

  1. Ask your HR team whether your company offers a matching gift program.
  2. Submit the match through your employer, naming Pathleticz (EIN: 42-2273307) as the recipient.
  3. Forward any confirmation or forms to give@pathleticz.com and we'll take care of our side.

Some companies also give based on the hours employees volunteer. Ask whether time you spend supporting Pathleticz programs qualifies.

Join the lineup

Companies and organizations can partner with Pathleticz to support programming, bring their people in as guest professionals, and connect directly with students building careers in the industry.

To start the conversation, email give@pathleticz.com or sign up under Sponsors & Partners on our homepage.
